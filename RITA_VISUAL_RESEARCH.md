# Rita visual research

Research date: 2026-09-23

## References reviewed

- Honkai Impact 3rd official site and guide index:
  https://honkaiimpact3.hoyoverse.com/
- Official Honkai Impact 3 Wiki — Umbral Rose and Dorm Chibi pages:
  https://honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/Umbral_Rose
  https://honkaiimpact3.fandom.com/wiki/Umbral_Rose/Dorm_Chibi
- Official community material on HoYoLAB, used only to corroborate costume and
  weapon vocabulary:
  https://www.hoyolab.com/article/1817465
- Public character imagery was reviewed through image search for silhouette and
  palette comparison. No searched image was downloaded into the project.

## Extracted visual language

- Short ash-taupe bob with an oversized side fringe covering one eye.
- One clear rose-magenta eye and a calm, controlled expression.
- Black and warm-ivory gothic maid silhouette with abundant but fine frills.
- Deep rose ornaments, black bow/headpiece, narrow ribbon tails.
- Scythe and rose-thorn motifs used as small silhouette accents.
- Chibi proportions: large head, compact torso, short limbs, readable pose at
  roughly 180–220 px.
- Personality direction: graceful, composed, slightly mysterious; tired states
  remain elegant instead of becoming loud or slapstick.

## Production decision

The shipped sheet was generated anew from this written brief using OpenAI image
generation, then inspected at desktop scale. It is not a crop, trace, extracted
game texture, or reproduction of any reference composition. The final pass
added transparent padding to prevent frame-edge clipping.
